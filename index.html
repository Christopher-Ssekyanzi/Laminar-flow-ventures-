TURNKEY EXECUTION PROCESS

From Geological Aquifer Strike to Elevated Reservoir

A documented photographic walkthrough of our full engineering lifecycle on site — delivering certified potable water to every point.

STAGE 01

Deep Drilling & Aquifer Strike

Rotary borehole drilling striking high-pressure subterranean aquifer flow with high-velocity water discharge and continuous yield logging.

STAGE 02

Casing & Sand-Free Well Completion

Lowering precision slotted uPVC hydro-screens with calibrated silica gravel packing to guarantee decades of sediment-free water.

STAGE 03

Steel Tower Fabrication & Erection

Reinforced concrete foundation casting, structural steel cutting, arc welding, and high-altitude tower framing under strict safety standards.

STAGE 04

Elevated 10,000L Gravity Reservoir

Mounting heavy-capacity storage reservoir with safety railings, solar pumping integration, and flanged gravity-feed distribution networks.
