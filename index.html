TURNKEY EXECUTION PROCESS

From Geological Aquifer Strike to Elevated Reservoir

A documented photographic walkthrough of our full engineering lifecycle on site.

STAGE 01

Deep Drilling & Aquifer Strike

Rotary borehole drilling striking high-pressure subterranean aquifer flow with high-velocity water discharge.

STAGE 02

Casing & Sand-Free Completion

Lowering slotted uPVC hydro-screens with silica gravel packing to guarantee decades of sediment-free water.

STAGE 03

Steel Tower Fabrication

Reinforced concrete foundation casting, structural steel cutting, arc welding, and tower framing.

Mounting heavy-capacity storage reservoir with safety railings and flanged gravity-feed distribution networks.
